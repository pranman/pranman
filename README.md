# Hey, I'm Pranay 👋

**Senior freelance engineer with a product brain and a creative streak.**

I take messy briefs through to useful, working software. Through [Big White Dog](https://www.bigwhitedog.dev/), I work across product direction, architecture and hands-on engineering.

In the past I've led engineering teams in insurance; if you get a motor, home, or commercial insurance quote in the UK, chances are some of the underlying tech was built or designed by me.

I branched my career into politics after the Brexit referendum and worked as CTO at Best for Britain, where I led our anti-Brexit digital campaigning, ran hackathons, built information apps, created and ran ads, in the process becoming the **[largest non-party political advertiser](https://search.electoralcommission.org.uk/Search/Spending?currentPage=1&rows=50&query=best%20for%20britain&sort=DateIncurred&order=desc&tab=1&open=filter&et=tp&includeOutsideSection75=true&evt=ukparliament&evt=nationalassemblyforwales&evt=scottishparliament&evt=northernirelandassembly&evt=europeanparliament&evt=senedd&evt=referendum&ev=3696&ev=3677&optCols=CampaigningName&optCols=ExpenseCategoryName&optCols=FullAddress&optCols=AmountInEngland&optCols=AmountInScotland&optCols=AmountInWales&optCols=AmountInNorthernIreland&optCols=DateOfClaimForPayment&optCols=DatePaid)** in the UK. I also built getvoting.org, our tactical voting website which ended up being visited by over 4.5 million people in the run-up to the election. This led to some **[significant wins](https://bestforbritain.org/publication/2019electionimpact/)**, albeit the campaign spending restrictions at the time did not quite allow us to have the election-winning effect we wanted.

### What keeps me busy

- 🛠️ **Product engineering** — commerce, booking systems and content platforms, from the first sketch to the bits that keep them running.
- 🤖 **AI & agents** — automated workflows and agentic development, with clear goals, human judgement and proper checks.
- 📈 **Growth & creative** — advertising, content and analytics. I care about what happens after launch, too.

I also run **[Papernaut](https://bsky.app/profile/ukpolitics.papernaut.co)**: an automated UK politics news feed that finds, groups and publishes stories, with a traceable editorial pipeline.
