# Hey, I'm Pranay 👋

**Technology leader, product builder and hands-on engineer.**

I turn messy, ambitious ideas into useful software — from product strategy and architecture through to writing the code and getting it into people's hands. Through [Big White Dog](https://www.bigwhitedog.dev/), I work across product, engineering, AI and growth.

I've led engineering teams and designed large-scale insurance quotation engines; if you've bought motor, home or commercial insurance in the UK, there's a reasonable chance you've interacted with technology I helped build or design.

#### Politics & social impact

After the Brexit referendum, I moved part of my career into politics, becoming CTO at **Best for Britain**. I led our digital campaigning against Brexit — building campaign technology, running hackathons, developing information tools and managing large-scale digital advertising. During this period, Best for Britain became the **[UK's largest non-party political advertiser](https://search.electoralcommission.org.uk/Search/Spending?currentPage=1&rows=50&query=best%20for%20britain&sort=DateIncurred&order=desc&tab=1&open=filter&et=tp&includeOutsideSection75=true&evt=ukparliament&evt=nationalassemblyforwales&evt=scottishparliament&evt=northernirelandassembly&evt=europeanparliament&evt=senedd&evt=referendum&ev=3696&ev=3677&optCols=CampaigningName&optCols=ExpenseCategoryName&optCols=FullAddress&optCols=AmountInEngland&optCols=AmountInScotland&optCols=AmountInWales&optCols=AmountInNorthernIreland&optCols=DateOfClaimForPayment&optCols=DatePaid)**.

For the 2019 general election, I built **GetVoting.org**, our tactical-voting platform. More than **4.5 million people** used it in the six weeks before polling day. Our post-election analysis found that more than 800,000 people voted tactically in line with our recommendations and that the campaign helped produce **[significant constituency-level wins](https://bestforbritain.org/publication/2019electionimpact/)**. Non-party campaign spending limits constrained how far we could scale the operation, so we ultimately fell short of the national result we were trying to achieve.

My involvement in social causes predates my professional political work. In 2015, my partner and I built **[Refugee Maps](https://refugeemaps.org/)**, a volunteer project helping refugees travelling through Europe find aid and avoid dangers such as landmines. It was covered by **[Slate](https://slate.com/technology/2015/09/mapping-technology-is-helping-the-european-refugees-avoid-land-mines-and-find-aid.html)** and **[The Guardian](https://www.theguardian.com/world/2015/sep/09/are-you-organising-an-event-supporting-refugees)**.

Earlier still, during the 2011 Libyan civil war, my father was among roughly 16,000 Indians stranded in Libya. I launched a campaign in India calling for their evacuation, documented at the time by **[Youth Ki Awaaz](https://www.youthkiawaaz.com/2011/02/indians-stuck-in-libya/)**. The campaign helped trigger the Indian government's response, which became **[Operation Safe Homecoming](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Operation_Safe_Homecoming)** — a military-led operation to evacuate Indian citizens from Libya.
